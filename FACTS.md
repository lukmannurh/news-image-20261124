## Lead — Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang

- Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday 23 Nov 2026 to reinstate co-founder
  Jensen Huang as chief executive, 11 days after he resigned (12 Nov) over revelations
  of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman US officials believe is a
  Chinese spy. Sources: Reuters (via BusinessWorld/TradingView, 24 Nov), Bloomberg Law,
  TMJ4/AP, Hypebeast.
- President Donald Trump pressed the board directly; he told CNBC "I made the deal"
  and "I convinced them". Source: Reuters/BBC reporting, 24 Nov.
- Shares soared more than 30% in a day (31% per Times of India; "more than 30%" per
  TMJ4/AP; "biggest rally in decades" per Bloomberg). Source: Reuters, Bloomberg, AP.
- Huang is due to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on 3 December 2026.
  Source: TMJ4/AP, 24 Nov.

## Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya

- Ukrainian naval drones sank one of Russia's newest warships in Libyan waters;
  Zelenskyy praised his security services on Monday 23 Nov. Kyiv said a corvette
  (reported as the "Mercury") and a Ka-27 helicopter were destroyed off Benghazi.
  Source: AP (via southpolitics.com, 24 Nov), Al Jazeera reporting.

## Zelenskyy: four-way peace talks "literally tomorrow"

- Zelenskyy told NATO parliamentarians in Berlin on Monday 23 Nov that Ukraine is
  ready to join four-way talks with Russia, Europe and the US "literally tomorrow";
  partners aim to hold the meeting before Christmas. Source: France24/Asha News
  (issued 24 Nov 01:30), AzerNews, MyUkraineIs/Ukrinform.
- The European Commission presented its 22nd sanctions package against Russia on
  Monday 23 Nov, targeting banks, crypto platforms, the shadow fleet, visas and
  Russian teams competing under the Russian flag. Source: h.ua, WH News, Euronews.

## CrowdStrike buys Zscaler for $44 billion

- CrowdStrike agreed on Monday 23 Nov to acquire Zscaler in a $43.9 billion
  all-stock deal, the biggest cybersecurity acquisition on record. Zscaler holders
  get 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per share, a ~34% premium; expected to close in the
  second half of 2027. Source: Reuters (23 Nov), TechCrunch, Fast Company.

- US Secretary of State Marco Rubio met Qatar's prime minister on Monday as part of
  the US-Qatar strategic dialogue. Source: AP (24 Nov).
